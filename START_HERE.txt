Stickman Hybrid: make stickman documentary explainers on any topic with Antigravity:
clean full-colour scenes plus cut-out stickers that pop in on the spoken words
(the style of the viral "ancient humans" and stick-figure explainer channels)

Before this, set up Flow Connector: open the extension's side panel, Setup -> Copy setup
prompt, and paste that into your Antigravity chat.

Then do ONE of these:

  A) Open this folder in Antigravity (as a workspace) and type in chat:
       start
     or:
       stickman hybrid start

  B) In any Antigravity chat, type:
       stickman-animation is in my Downloads folder. Read GEMINI.md and follow the one-time setup.

Antigravity installs/syncs the skills globally (~/.gemini/config/skills/), verifies
your environment (Node.js, Flow Connector, FFmpeg with libass, Python packages, sound kit),
and guides you through making your video. Afterwards it works from ANY chat:
    stickman hybrid start        a new video
    stickman hybrid continue     carry on where you stopped

You choose the topic (or ask for 10 ideas), the language and the length. Antigravity
picks the look that suits the topic (bright, warm, ink, painted or night) and
shows you three checkpoints:
  1. The script & expressive tags
  2. The voice (VO.wav merge or automated VO)
  3. The final video

(Agents: read GEMINI.md and README.md in this folder and follow them.)
