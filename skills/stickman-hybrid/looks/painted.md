# painted

**When:**
- second-person survival stories and serials ("You have four litres of water left...")
- disasters, war, expeditions

The pattern is one recurring hero, painted places and cut-out sticker props.

**STYLE** (paste this after `look: painted`):

2D illustrated graphic-novel style, detailed hand-painted backgrounds with realistic perspective and muted natural colours (stone greys, cool blues, rust, faded greens), soft cinematic light, a full-bleed scene that fills the whole frame, 16:9. Characters are simple stick figures drawn in clean ink: a round white head with two small black dot eyes and a simple mouth, thin black stick limbs, with real clothing items and accessories drawn over the stick body.

**Character sheet:**
- One hero with an accessory you cannot miss, such as a mustard beanie and a grey scarf.
- One to three companions, each told apart by one clothing colour or one prop.

**Notes:**
- Stats, countdowns, and facts are spoken by the narrator, never written on screen. The screen shows the hero, the environment, and cut-out prop stickers.
- Keep the same place for several lines in a row and change only what the hero does.
  Painted places cost the most to get right.
